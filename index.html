MetricHealthy looks like
How each number is measured
Failure rate
System-caused failed attempts ÷ total attempted transactions × 100. Source: POS logs and incident records. Customer and card-issuer declines are excluded.
Catalog accuracy
Correct audited product records ÷ records sampled × 100. Source: POS catalog against approved source data.
Reconciliation
Business days reconciled ÷ business days elapsed × 100. Source: POS reports, processor settlement reports, store records.
Repeat support
Repeat issue tickets per 100 transactions against the first 30-day baseline. Source: ticket log and POS volume.
Manual-price share
Manually priced or custom-price sale lines ÷ all sale lines × 100. A rising share means cashiers no longer trust the catalog. Targets for this one are my proposal.
